Yinuo Xuanyuan

I am an artist and graphic designer, and graduated from the MA programme at Werkplaats Typografie in Arnhem in 2026.

My work focuses on miscommunication within intimate and emotional contexts. It uses sound, image, and text to explore how meaning shifts, fragments, or remains unspoken—where closeness and distance exist at the same time, and understanding is constantly being renegotiated.
